About the creator

Hi, I’m Kris.
Call me KNIGHT.

I’m a practical problem-solver who turned a love of useful software into a growing collection of apps.

Kris Tan · KNIGHTCreator, tinkerer & lifelong learner

From fixing PCs to building apps.

Before I made apps, I worked in customer service and content moderation. My strongest hands-on skill was—and still is—working with PCs: troubleshooting, taking them apart, and putting them back together.

For a long time, I thought programming was out of reach. I used to joke that my coding ability was about half a percent. But I’ve always loved designing apps and utilities I’d genuinely use myself.

AI changed what felt possible. I don’t see it as a replacement for ideas or care; I see it as a tool I can direct, question, and refine. I bring the problems, the product instincts, and the persistence. AI helps me turn those ideas into working software—then I test, improve, and share what I make.

“I make tools I wish I already had—and I’m learning something new with every release.”

These projects are a work in progress, made in public and shared freely. If one saves you time or makes your day a little easier, that’s the best kind of feedback.
